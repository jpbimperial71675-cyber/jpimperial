Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Why is the menu loop capped at 20 seconds? Isn't that too short?

It is not too short—it is scientifically proven for high-foot-traffic food courts.

According to retail buying behavior, hungry customers walking past a food stall have an attention span of only 3 to 7 seconds. If a video runs for 60 seconds, customers have to wait too long to see what they want, causing them to walk away to a neighboring stall.

The 20-second loop (4 Hero Highlights + 6 Menu Items) is the golden ratio to trigger appetite and drive immediate orders at the counter.

❓ Why do I need a Menu Guard Retainer if I can just copy the file to a USB myself?

Copying a file sounds simple, but managing it yourself introduces severe operational risks:

  • TV Codec Errors: A minor wrong export setting from a phone or editing tool can trigger an Unsupported Format warning or black screen on your TV right during peak lunch rush.
  • Physical USB Loss: Due to continuous heat and operation in food stalls, USB drives corrupt. Under our retainer, we replace them immediately with zero business disruption.
  • Margin Protection: Meat or ingredient prices change? Simply message us to update your price badges before profit margins are squeezed.
